The problem

Warranties quietly expire. Money quietly disappears.

Most people lose track of their coverage weeks after buying. When something breaks, the receipt is gone and the warranty window is closed.

Paper receipts fade — or vanish

Thermal receipts turn blank, emails get buried, and store portals are painful to dig through.

Extended warranties expire silently

You paid for coverage you forgot to use — because nothing told you the deadline was close.

Documents need renewals too

Passports, IDs, insurance policies and licenses all expire — usually at the worst possible moment.

Filing a claim is a scavenger hunt

Finding the receipt, the product details and the purchase date turns a 10-minute task into a day of searching.

The fix

MyWarranty fixes all four.

Scan a receipt once — MyWarranty reads it, files it, watches the clock, and reminds you before anything expires. Your vault is backed up to your account, so a lost phone never means lost coverage.

Features

Built to make forgetting impossible

One vault for everything

Warranties live next to expiring documents — passport, ID card, insurance, driver's license, visa. Search, sort, filter, and see status at a glance: active, expiring soon, or expired.

Warranties + documents in one place

Scan a receipt — the rest is automatic

Point the camera at any receipt. On-device OCR reads the store, product, date and total, then pre-fills the form. Everything is processed on the phone — no uploads needed for scanning.

OCR scan → auto-filled details

Reminders that never sleep

Get notified 30, 7 and 1 day before every expiry, at 9:00 — for warranties and documents alike. Scheduled on your phone and pushed from the cloud, so you're warned even with the app closed.

Phone + cloud push, even with the app closed

Cloud backup & real-time sync

Sign in and your vault is backed up automatically. New phone? Sign in and everything is back. Changes sync in real time, and deletions propagate safely across devices.

New phone → sign in → everything restored

Claim wizard with generated letters

Something broke? Describe what went wrong and MyWarranty drafts your claim: a professional PDF letter with product details and receipt embedded — or plain text for email. History kept per item.

PDF claim letter, receipt attached

Stats dashboard

See the value you're protecting: items tracked, total value, expiring soon, already expired — plus value by category and where you spend the most, broken down by store.

Know what your coverage is worth

Calendar view

A month grid shows every expiry as a color-coded dot — green (active), amber (expiring), red (expired). Tap any day to see exactly what expires then.

Your deadlines, at a glance

Export, restore & full control

Export a CSV, a formatted PDF report, or a complete JSON backup. Restore at any time — and deleting your account wipes your cloud data and local files for good.

How it works

Three steps. Then forget about it.

1

Scan or add

Snap the receipt — OCR fills in the store, product, date and price. Or add any item manually in seconds.

2

Set the expiry

Pick the warranty term or the document's expiry date. Status colors and the calendar take over from there.

3

Relax

Reminders arrive at 30, 7 and 1 day — on time, every time. File a claim with one tap if anything breaks.

Privacy & personalization

Private by design. Yours in every language.

App lock

Protect the vault with a 4-digit PIN and fingerprint unlock. The app locks automatically 10 seconds after you leave it — and after too many wrong attempts, it locks itself out.

Your account, your data

Every account can only ever read its own vault. Receipt photos stay on your device — they are not uploaded. Deleting your account removes your cloud data, local files and the account itself.
